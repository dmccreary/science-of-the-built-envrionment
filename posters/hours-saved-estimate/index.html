    Hours Saved Estimate¶

    This page estimates how many human hours an artist, a graphic designer, and an instructional designer would have needed to create the 20 interactive infographic posters in this book. It is a judgment-based estimate built from typical production rates for each role. It is not measured.

    The estimated total is about 790 specialist hours, or roughly 835 human hours if the instructor's time in the weekly meetings is counted. A reasonable range is 600 to 1,100 specialist hours. At typical market rates that is about $54,000 of specialist labor, within a range of roughly $21,000 to $107,000.

    Work by Role¶

    Role Work Hours
    Instructional designer Research and select the 20 frameworks, about 30 hours. Per poster (about 9 hours each): learning objectives, chapter alignment, 5 to 12 region descriptions with 3 to 5 facts each, 8 to 12 quiz questions, source checking, and the art brief. Review cycles, about 20 hours. ~230
    Artist 20 detailed technical illustrations, each with a different composition and no reused scene. About 14 hours each: sketch, line work, color, revisions to match the brief, and a character cameo. ~280
    Graphic designer Style system, palette, type and layout rules, about 20 hours. Per poster (about 4 hours): composition review and region layout. Overlay integration and calibration, about 30 hours. Thumbnails, export and QA, about 20 hours. ~150
    Subtotal ~660

    Meetings¶

    The three specialists would meet with the instructor 4 hours a week. The artist is the bottleneck. The designer and instructional designer work ahead of and alongside the artist, so the estimate assumes about 11 calendar weeks. That is roughly 7 weeks of artist production plus sequencing and review cycles.

    Item Hours
    3 specialists × 4 hours × 11 weeks ~132
    Instructor's own time in those meetings (not specialist time) ~44

    Totals¶

    Hours
    Specialist hours (work plus meetings) ~790
    Including instructor meeting time ~835

    What This Leaves Out¶

    • Project management, licensing, and accessibility review.
    • Instructor review time outside the weekly meetings.
    • Extra concept rounds if the instructor changes direction mid-project.

    The range goes up if the art has to be hand-drawn at the technical accuracy in the image prompts, with several revision rounds per poster. It goes down if the artist works from stock vector assets or the team reuses scenes across posters.

    Hourly Rates¶

    Rates vary widely by source, seniority, and region, and none of these figures is specific to Minneapolis. The scenarios below use three rate levels for each role.

    Role Low Typical High Basis
    Instructional designer $38 $70 $100 Low is the reported average freelance pay. Typical is an established freelancer. High is a senior specialist.
    Graphic designer $34 $65 $90 Low is the reported average employee or freelance wage. Typical is a mid-level designer. High is a senior designer.
    Artist (technical illustrator) $34 $70 $100 Low is the reported average technical illustrator wage. Typical is a mid-to-senior freelance illustrator. High is a senior illustrator.

    The low rates are average wages, so they understate what a team would charge as contractors. The typical rates are the most realistic for a commissioned project. The rate ranges come from these sources:

    • ZipRecruiter: Freelance Instructional Designer salary
    • Experiencing Elearning: Instructional Design Hourly Rates and Salary
    • Freelance Pricing: Graphic Designer Rate Guide
    • ZipRecruiter: Freelance Graphic Designer salary
    • Salary.com: Technical Illustrator salary
    • ZipRecruiter: Freelance Illustrator salary

    Total Cost Saved¶

    Each role's hours are its work hours plus its 44 meeting hours (4 hours a week for about 11 weeks). The low and high scenarios scale every role's hours to the 600 and 1,100 totals.

    Scenario Instructional designer Graphic designer Artist Total hours Total cost
    Low 207 h × $38 = $7,866 147 h × $34 = $4,998 245 h × $34 = $8,330 599 $21,194
    Typical 274 h × $70 = $19,180 194 h × $65 = $12,610 324 h × $70 = $22,680 792 $54,470
    High 381 h × $100 = $38,100 269 h × $90 = $24,210 450 h × $100 = $45,000 1,100 $107,310

    The typical estimate is about $54,000 saved, with a plausible range of about $21,000 to $107,000.

    Read this as a gross figure for the labor that was avoided:

    • It does not subtract the cost of the AI tools used, or the author's own time reviewing and revising the generated posters.
    • The instructor's 44 hours in meetings are not priced, because that is faculty time and not specialist time.
    • It does not include project management, licensing, or accessibility review.
