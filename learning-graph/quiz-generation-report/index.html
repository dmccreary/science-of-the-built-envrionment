    Distractor Length Check¶

    A first pass found that the correct option was the longest in 78% of questions, which lets a test-wise student guess without knowing the content. A revision pass lengthened distractors and trimmed correct options. After revision, the average correct option is about 5% longer than the average distractor, and the correct option is still the longest or tied for longest in roughly half of the questions.

    Scoring Note¶

    The quality score is a heuristic computed from format and link checks (20), concept coverage (20), Bloom's alignment (up to 25), answer balance (up to 15), distractor length parity (up to 10), and explanation length (up to 10). It does not measure whether each question is unambiguous or whether each distractor is plausible. Those judgments were made by the generating model while writing the questions and have not had an independent human or second-model review.
