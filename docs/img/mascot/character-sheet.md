# Character Sheet: Beau the Beaver

The canonical identity document for Beau, the pedagogical
mascot for the **The Science of the Built Environment** textbook. Every pose prompt and
every piece of AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to
the description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Beau
- **Species:** Beaver
- **Subject:** Building science and construction
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's build it right!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Chestnut brown — hex `#8B5A2B`
- **Accent color:** Safety orange — hex `#F57C00`
- **Clothing / accessories:** Safety-orange hard hat and a small tan tool belt with a tape measure
- **Expression:** Large, kind eyes and a friendly, capable expression
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact, suitable for icon-sized display
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector cartoon with clean lines

## Personality

- Practical and hands-on
- Patient and encouraging
- Safety-minded without being preachy
- Curious about how things are put together

## Voice

- Uses plain, jobsite-friendly language and explains terms the first time they appear
- Occasionally uses a building pun, such as "load-bearing advice" or "solid foundation"
- Refers to students as "builders" or "apprentices"
- Signature phrases: "Let's build it right!", "Check your load path!", "Measure twice, build once!"

## Why This Mascot

Beavers are nature's builders: they select materials, manage water, and create durable structures, which matches the book's themes of materials, enclosure, and durability. Beavers are also a familiar Minnesota animal, and the hard hat and tool belt tie the character to the construction trades Dunwoody students are entering.
