# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

*The Science of the Built Environment* is a textbook about how building materials, structural physics, enclosure assemblies, mechanical/electrical/plumbing (MEP) systems, building codes, and sustainable practices combine to create safe, durable, and energy-efficient buildings, with a special focus on cold-climate building science. The intended audience is undergraduate college students in construction, architecture, and building systems programs (including future electrical designers) at Dunwoody College of Technology and similar institutions. The visual tone should be modern, technically grounded, and scientifically precise, balancing architectural engineering clarity with visual warmth and curiosity.

## Title

Place **The Science of the Built Environment** in the center of the image, in a clean, bold, highly legible sans-serif font. Use a crisp white/light cream font color with a subtle drop shadow and a semi-translucent dark scrim directly behind the lettering so it stays completely readable against the surrounding montage background. Keep the title prominently sized; maintain generous negative space behind and around the text block.

## Background Montage

Arrange a montage of the following 8 concepts around the central title in an orbit, each rendered in a consistent isometric architectural illustration style with clean linework and technical elegance:

1. **Structural Load Paths & Framing**: An isometric cutaway of heavy timber framing and structural steel members with subtle color-coded vector arrows demonstrating how vertical gravity loads, lateral wind loads, and snow loads transfer through beams, columns, and shear walls down to the foundation.
2. **Building Enclosure Control Layers**: An exploded architectural cross-section of a high-performance exterior wall showing the four essential control layers in sequence: rainscreen cladding, water-resistive barrier (WRB), continuous rigid exterior insulation, air barrier, cavity insulation, and interior vapor control.
3. **Building Physics & Thermal Dynamics**: A color-coded thermal gradient heatmap cross-section of a building envelope corner, showing heat flux, isotherm lines, and moisture/vapor migration under extreme winter exterior conditions versus comfortable indoor conditions.
4. **Site Soils & Cold-Climate Foundations**: A stratified ground cross-section detailing frost depth, showing a frost-protected concrete footing, perimeter drain tile, gravel drainage bed, and deep structural piles resting on bedrock.
5. **Mechanical Systems & Ventilation**: A clean 3D mechanical schematic showing an Energy Recovery Ventilator (ERV) and ducted air paths with blue fresh-air supply loops and red stale-exhaust air streams distributed across a building volume.
6. **Electrical Service & Lighting Distribution**: An isometric blueprint-style depiction of a main electrical distribution panel with orderly conduit risers, branch circuit routing, and energy-efficient architectural LED lighting grids.
7. **Architectural Blueprints & Working Drawings**: Architectural drafting sheets showing dimensional floor plans, section elevation callouts, grid lines, and graphic symbols neatly organized on a drafting table.
8. **Sustainable Mass Timber & High Performance**: Cross-laminated timber (CLT) panels and curved glulam timber beams integrated with a green living roof and high-efficiency rooftop photovoltaic solar panels.

## Style & Composition

- **Illustration style**: Modern isometric technical illustration with crisp architectural linework, precise cutaways, and clean vector shapes. All montage elements must share this consistent aesthetic so the composition reads as a unified whole.
- **Color palette**: Deep architectural navy/slate blue (`#1E293B`) for the background field and structure, warm Douglas-fir timber amber/honey (`#D97706`), safety orange accents (`#F57C00`) tying into the mascot's hard hat, and fresh environmental sage/emerald green (`#059669`) for sustainable features.
- **Lighting/mood**: Bright, crisp architectural daylighting with clean dimensional highlights and subtle depth gradients, optimistic and forward-looking.
- **Composition**: Title centered horizontally and vertically; the 8 montage vignettes arranged in a balanced orbit around the perimeter; mascot Beau welcoming the viewer from the lower-left corner; clear visual hierarchy with high contrast ensuring title legibility.

## Avoid

- Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible fake text or messy scribbles anywhere in the image.
- Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (isolated glowing lightbulbs, business handshakes, generic graphs with meaningless upward arrows).
- Avoid photorealistic human faces or distracting photographic cutouts.
- Do not let montage diagrams visually compete with, run through, or obscure the title text.
