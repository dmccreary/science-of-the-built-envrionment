# Durability, Maintenance, and Building Failure

## Summary

What determines a building's service life, how buildings fail from water, movement, and decay, and how they are assessed, renovated, and reused. It builds on the prerequisite concepts from Chapters 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11, 19, 20. After completing this chapter, students will be able to define, explain, and apply the 20 concepts listed below.
