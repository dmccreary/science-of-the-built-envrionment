# Chapters

This textbook is organized into 21 chapters covering 380 concepts.

## Chapter Overview

1. [Introduction to the Built Environment and Construction Terminology](01-intro-terminology/index.md) - How buildings, materials, and systems are defined and described, including the drawings, specifications, and vocabulary of the construction industry.
2. [The Design and Construction Process](02-design-construction-process/index.md) - The phases of a project from programming through closeout, the delivery methods, and the roles of the owner, architect, contractor, and subcontractors.
3. [Forces, Heat, and the Physics of Buildings](03-forces-heat-physics/index.md) - The forces, stress, strain, heat transfer, and thermal resistance principles that explain how buildings carry loads and resist heat flow.
4. [Moisture, Air Movement, and Thermal Comfort](04-moisture-air-comfort/index.md) - How moisture, humidity, pressure differences, and air leakage move through buildings, and how they affect condensation and comfort.
5. [Properties of Building Materials](05-material-properties/index.md) - The mechanical, thermal, moisture, and fire properties that distinguish building materials, and how materials are tested and selected.
6. [Structural Loads and Load Paths](06-structural-loads/index.md) - How dead, live, snow, wind, and seismic loads travel through beams, columns, walls, and bracing to the ground.
7. [Wood and Steel Framing](07-wood-steel-framing/index.md) - The lumber, engineered wood, and structural steel systems used for framing, along with their connections and typical assemblies.
8. [Concrete and Masonry](08-concrete-masonry/index.md) - The ingredients, mixing, placing, and reinforcing of concrete, and the units, mortar, and grout used in masonry construction.
9. [Site Work, Soils, and Groundwater](09-site-soils/index.md) - How site analysis, excavation, soil properties, groundwater, and frost conditions shape what can be built on a site.
10. [Foundation Systems](10-foundation-systems/index.md) - The shallow and deep foundations, foundation walls, slabs, and retaining walls that transfer building loads to the soil.
11. [Enclosure Control Layers and Insulation](11-enclosure-insulation/index.md) - The water, air, vapor, and thermal control layers of wall assemblies, and the insulation materials that provide thermal control in a cold climate.
12. [Cladding, Windows, Doors, and Air Sealing](12-cladding-windows-air-sealing/index.md) - The cladding systems, flashing, windows, doors, and air-sealing methods that complete a wall, plus the interior finishes behind them.
13. [Roof Assemblies](13-roof-assemblies/index.md) - How low-slope and steep-slope roofs shed water, drain, ventilate, and resist ice dams, including green roofs.
14. [HVAC, Plumbing, and Fire Protection Systems](14-hvac-plumbing-fire/index.md) - The heating, cooling, ventilation, plumbing, and fire protection systems that make a building habitable and safe.
15. [Electrical Fundamentals and Building Service](15-electrical-fundamentals/index.md) - Voltage, current, power, and the equipment that brings electricity from the utility into a building.
16. [Electrical Distribution, Lighting, and Design Team Coordination](16-electrical-distribution-design/index.md) - Wiring, lighting, low-voltage, and renewable systems, and how the electrical designer works with other disciplines through each project phase.
17. [Building Codes, Permits, and Enforcement](17-building-codes-permits/index.md) - How model codes and the Minnesota State Building Code are adopted, applied, and enforced through permits, plan review, and inspection.
18. [Fire Protection and Life Safety Requirements](18-fire-life-safety/index.md) - The code requirements for occupancy, construction type, fire-resistance ratings, egress, and emergency power that protect occupants.
19. [Energy Efficiency and High-Performance Buildings](19-energy-efficiency/index.md) - How energy codes, passive design, and rating systems lead to efficient, high-performance, and net-zero energy buildings.
20. [Sustainable Building Materials](20-sustainable-materials/index.md) - How embodied carbon, life-cycle assessment, and material health are used to compare and select sustainable building materials.
21. [Durability, Maintenance, and Building Failure](21-durability-failure/index.md) - What determines a building's service life, how buildings fail from water, movement, and decay, and how they are assessed, renovated, and reused.

## How to Use This Textbook

The chapters build from the physics and materials of buildings, through structure, enclosure, and building systems, to codes, sustainability, and durability. Dependencies are respected, so each concept is introduced after the concepts it relies on. Read the chapters in order the first time through.

---

**Note:** Each chapter includes a list of concepts covered. Make sure to complete prerequisites before moving to advanced chapters.
