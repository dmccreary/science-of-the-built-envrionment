# References: Foundation Systems

1. [Foundation (engineering)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundation_(engineering)) - Wikipedia - Explains how foundations transfer structure loads to the ground, from historic stone and rubble-trench types to modern shallow and deep systems, giving students the vocabulary and classification this chapter builds on.

2. [Piling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piling) - Wikipedia - Surveys driven piles, drilled piers, and specialty piles, with materials and construction machinery, directly supporting the chapter's deep foundations material for sites where near-surface soils cannot carry building loads.

3. [Retaining wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retaining_wall) - Wikipedia - Describes gravity, cantilever, anchored, and sheet-pile retaining walls and the lateral earth pressure they resist, stressing drainage behind the wall, which ties directly to the chapter's retaining wall and drainage sections.

4. Building Construction Illustrated (6th Edition) - Francis D. K. Ching - Wiley - Ching's measured axonometric and section drawings of footings, foundation walls, slabs, and piles show each component in its true relationship to soil and frame, making foundation assemblies readable at a glance.

5. Soil Mechanics in Engineering Practice (3rd Edition) - Karl Terzaghi, Ralph B. Peck, and Gholamreza Mesri - Wiley - Terzaghi's effective stress principle and Peck's observational method underpin modern bearing capacity and settlement reasoning, explaining why footing size and depth follow from soil behavior rather than rules of thumb.

6. [Minnesota Rule 1303.1600: Footing Depth for Frost Protection](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/1303.1600/) - Minnesota Office of the Revisor of Statutes - The state rule setting minimum footing depths by county zone, with an engineer-justified exception and slab-on-grade provisions, showing students the legal frost-depth requirement behind cold-climate footing design.

7. [Footing Drain Pipe](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/footing-drain-pipe) - Building America Solution Center, U.S. Department of Energy - Gives installation details for perforated footing drains, including gravel cover, fabric wrap, slope, and discharge distance, with right-and-wrong photo comparisons that illustrate the chapter's foundation drainage concept.

8. [Damp-Proof Exterior Surface of Below-Grade Walls](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/damp-proof-exterior-surface-below-grade-walls) - Building America Solution Center, U.S. Department of Energy - Covers coatings for poured concrete and masonry foundation walls, plus grading, drains, and cold-climate frost heave and adfreeze cautions, linking foundation walls and basements to moisture control.

9. [RR-0206: Foundations - Moisture Resistant Construction](https://buildingscience.com/documents/reports/rr-0206-foundations-moisture-resistant-construction/view) - Building Science Corporation - Lists the functions every foundation must perform and compares crawl space, slab-on-grade, and basement strategies, including treating crawl spaces as conditioned mini-basements, which informs the chapter's crawl space discussion.

10. [Foundation Capillary Break over Aggregate](https://basc.pnnl.gov/building-science-measures/foundation-capillary-break-over-aggregate) - Building America Solution Center, U.S. Department of Energy - Describes a taped polyethylene or rigid-foam layer over four inches of gravel beneath slabs and crawl space floors, a practical detail for keeping slab-on-grade and basement floors dry.
