# References: Battery Storage and Grid-Interactive Buildings

1. [Grid energy storage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grid_energy_storage) - Wikipedia - Surveys battery, mechanical, thermal, and chemical storage and the roles storage plays on the grid. Supports this appendix's battery energy storage concept, including short-duration lithium-ion suitability, pumped-hydro efficiency, and falling cost trends.

2. [Lithium iron phosphate battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate_battery) - Wikipedia - Explains LFP cell chemistry, thermal stability, and cycle life compared with nickel-based lithium-ion cells. Relevant to the appendix's chemistry and safety discussion, with energy-density trade-offs and LFP's large share of stationary storage.

3. [Vehicle-to-grid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle-to-grid) - Wikipedia - Describes how electric vehicles can feed power to homes, buildings, and the grid. Supports the appendix's vehicle-to-home concept, distinguishing V2H, V2B, and V2L and noting that system round-trip efficiency drops below the battery's own.

4. Sustainable Energy - Without the Hot Air (1st Edition) - David J. C. MacKay - UIT Cambridge - MacKay put every energy quantity in one everyday unit, kilowatt-hours per day per person. Chapter 26 uses it to size storage (about 20 kWh per person) and weigh electric-vehicle batteries against pumped hydro.

5. Linden's Handbook of Batteries (4th Edition) - Thomas B. Reddy (editor), David Linden (editor emeritus) - McGraw-Hill - No single originator stands out for this appendix's concepts, so this is chosen as the field's standard, most widely used battery reference. It compiles cell chemistry, rating, and performance data across battery types.

6. [Solar-Plus-Storage 101](https://www.energy.gov/eere/solar/articles/solar-plus-storage-101) - U.S. Department of Energy, Solar Energy Technologies Office - Explains how a battery charged by solar supplies evening and outage power. Useful for energy-versus-power sizing, with a 240 MWh, 60 MW example and a DC-coupled versus AC-coupled comparison.

7. [National Roadmap for Grid-Interactive Efficient Buildings](https://gebroadmap.lbl.gov/) - U.S. Department of Energy and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory - Lays out how buildings can shift and shed load to serve the grid. Supports the grid-interactive buildings concept, with a goal of tripling demand flexibility by 2030 and 14 recommendations.

8. [Battery Storage and Solar for Homes](https://mn.gov/commerce/energy/consumer/energy-programs/resources.jsp) - Minnesota Department of Commerce - Minnesota's consumer hub for home batteries and solar. Relevant to this appendix's battery energy storage concept, with a glossary defining a battery energy storage system, incentive listings, and links to installer directories.

9. [UL 9540A Test Method for Battery Energy Storage Systems](https://www.ul.com/services/ul-9540a-test-method) - UL Solutions - Describes the fire-propagation test method for thermal runaway at cell, module, unit, and installation levels. Supports this appendix's battery safety standards concept and its link to NFPA 855 and UL 9540 compliance.

10. [PUC approves Xcel's new optional time-of-use rate for residential customers](https://cubminnesota.org/puc-approves-xcels-new-optional-time-use-rate-residential-customers) - Citizens Utility Board of Minnesota - Reports Minnesota's approved voluntary residential time-of-use rate. Illustrates this appendix's time-of-use concept locally, with a 6 to 9 p.m. peak and peak prices 2.2 to 2.7 times off-peak.
