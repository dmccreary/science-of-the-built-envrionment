# References: Solar Photovoltaics

1. [Solar inverter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_inverter) - Wikipedia - Explains how inverters convert panel DC into grid-compatible AC. Supports this appendix's inverter concept, with sections on maximum power point tracking, grid-tied safety features, and string versus micro-inverter designs.

2. [Net metering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_metering) - Wikipedia - Covers how utilities credit customers for exported solar power and the controversy over replacing one-for-one credit. Includes the history of Minnesota's early 1983 net metering law and successor tariffs.

3. [Solar irradiance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_irradiance) - Wikipedia - Defines irradiance and insolation, including the roughly 1,000 W/m2 clear-sky surface value behind the peak sun hours concept. Explains the kWh/m2 units used in solar power sizing calculations.

4. Solar Engineering of Thermal Processes, Photovoltaics and Wind (5th Edition) - John A. Duffie, William A. Beckman, and Nate Blair - Wiley - Duffie and Beckman's group developed the f-chart method and the utilizability statistic, which size solar systems from monthly average radiation. The 5th edition adds PV chapters supporting sun-hours-based sizing.

5. Photovoltaic Systems Engineering (4th Edition) - Roger A. Messenger and Amir Abtahi - CRC Press - No single originator stands out for this appendix's concepts, so this is chosen as the PV text most praised for clarity. Reviewers cite its organization and worked designs of real systems.

6. [PVWatts Calculator](https://pvwatts.nlr.gov/) - National Laboratory of the Rockies (formerly NREL) - Free tool estimating energy production of grid-connected PV systems from location, array size, tilt, and a System Losses setting. It is the real-site check the appendix recommends over its illustrative 4.3 peak sun hours.

7. [Solar Integration: Inverters and Grid Services Basics](https://www.energy.gov/eere/solar/solar-integration-inverters-and-grid-services-basics) - U.S. Department of Energy Solar Energy Technologies Office - Describes how inverters convert DC to AC and compares central, string, and microinverters. Also covers smart inverter grid services such as voltage regulation and frequency response.

8. [Solar Radiation Basics](https://www.energy.gov/eere/solar/solar-radiation-basics) - U.S. Department of Energy Solar Energy Technologies Office - Explains direct, diffuse, and global radiation and why season and latitude matter. Notes that Denver gets nearly three times more solar energy in June than December, supporting the appendix's monthly production explorer.

9. [Minnesota Statutes 216B.164: Cogeneration and Small Power Production](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/216B.164) - Minnesota Office of the Revisor of Statutes - The law governing net metering in Minnesota. Sets the 40 kW and 1,000 kW thresholds, annual credit carryover and reset, and alternative distributed solar value tariffs.

10. [Residential Solar Systems](https://www.ag.state.mn.us/Consumer/Publications/ResidentialSolarSystems.asp) - Minnesota Attorney General's Office - Consumer guide to buying rooftop solar. Covers checking roof condition and lifespan, system sizing, net metering policies, contractor licensing, and the three-day cancellation right, echoing this appendix's roof-readiness warning.
