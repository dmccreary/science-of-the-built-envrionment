# Concept Taxonomy

The 448 concepts are grouped into 13 categories. No category exceeds 30% of the concepts. The first 380 concepts come from the chapters, and the last 68 come from the appendices.

## Foundations and Terminology (FOUND)

Core vocabulary, building types, and how to read drawings and specifications. (22 concepts)

## Design and Construction Process (PROC)

Project phases, delivery methods, and the roles each professional plays, including the electrical designer. (31 concepts)

## Building Physics (PHYS)

Forces, heat, moisture, air, light, and sound as they act on buildings. (28 concepts)

## Material Properties (MATP)

Strength, stiffness, durability, thermal, moisture, and fire properties and how materials are tested and selected. (22 concepts)

## Site and Foundations (SITE)

Soils, site work, frost, drainage, and foundation systems. (29 concepts)

## Structural Systems (STRC)

Loads, load paths, and wood, steel, concrete, and masonry structural systems. (64 concepts)

## Building Enclosure (ENCL)

Control layers, walls, roofs, insulation, windows, and air sealing. (47 concepts)

## Mechanical, Plumbing, and Fire Systems (MECH)

HVAC, plumbing, fire protection, and vertical transportation. (27 concepts)

## Electrical Systems (ELEC)

Electrical fundamentals, distribution, lighting, and low-voltage systems, with the electrical designer's work. (36 concepts)

## Codes and Regulations (CODE)

Building codes, the Minnesota State Building Code, permits, inspections, fire and life safety. (27 concepts)

## Sustainable Materials and Design (SUST)

Embodied carbon, life-cycle assessment, energy efficiency, and green building. (29 concepts)

## Durability and Failure (DURA)

Service life, maintenance, failure modes, forensics, and renovation. (18 concepts)

## Rapidly Evolving Technologies (EVOL)

Concepts from the appendices on fast-changing building technologies: heat pumps and refrigerants, heat recovery ventilation, ground-source and earth-coupled systems, solar photovoltaics and net metering, battery storage and grid-interactive buildings, smart sensors and building automation, mass timber and low-carbon materials, evolving energy codes, and prefabrication, digital design, and AI. Each concept depends on concepts taught in the chapters. (68 concepts)
