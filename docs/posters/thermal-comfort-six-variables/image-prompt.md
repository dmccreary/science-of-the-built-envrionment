## Layout Notes

- Six spokes, six badges. The badges are equal in size and evenly spaced around the person, and each has a different color and a different pictogram, so color never carries meaning alone.
- Markers are staggered on purpose: six sit high (about 24% to 48% from the top) and five sit low (about 56% to 92%). Keep cream space clear at those heights.
- Light comes from the upper left, line weight is consistent, and corner rounding is the same everywhere.
- The three scenes must look like three different places with identical wall thermostats in the two left scenes, because the lesson is that the same thermostat setting can give different comfort.
- The core idea is that the thermostat is only one of six variables, and the outdoor climate and the person's own choices shift the result.

## Do Not Include

- Any text, letters, numbers, labels, or captions
- A title, heading, or legend
- Arrows with text, signs with readable words, or numbers on any gauge
- Photorealistic people with faces, and any face other than Beau's
- Brand names, logos, a watermark, or a signature
