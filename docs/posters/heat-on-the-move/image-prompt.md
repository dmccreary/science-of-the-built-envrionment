## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind.** No title, no headings, no labels, no letters, no numerals, no captions, no arrows with writing, no logos, no watermark, and no signs with readable writing. The false-color thermal image must not include a temperature scale with numbers or a color-bar legend; if a color gradient strip appears, it must be a plain unlabeled gradient. All names, descriptions, and the title are added later by an interactive HTML overlay driven by a separate data file. If any lettering or numbers appear anywhere in the picture, regenerate.

## Image Specifications

- **Format:** PNG
- **Dimensions:** 1536 × 1024 px, landscape, 3:2 ratio
- **Background:** Warm cream (#FBF6EC) with a very faint blueprint grid
- **Audience:** College undergraduates in construction, architecture, and building-systems programs
- **Style:** Clean modern flat-vector technical illustration with soft shading and a consistent line weight, saturated but professional colors, in the same family as a flat cartoon mascot. Technically accurate building components. No photorealism and no 3D render look; the thermal image is drawn as a flat false-color illustration, not a photograph.

## What to Draw

One poster split into a **left half and a right half**, made of **six clearly separated panels**. Every panel is a rounded rectangle with a thin colored border and a slightly lighter cream fill, separated from its neighbors by a clear gap of cream (at least 0.8% of the image) so the panels read as distinct, separable regions. Nothing in a panel may cross its border.

- **Left half, three vignettes stacked in one column:** how heat moves in three ways, drawn as three equal-height panels. In reading order from the top: heat through a solid, heat carried by moving air, and heat as waves.
- **Right half, one large panel on top and two small panels beneath:** a false-color thermal image of an exterior wall, and under it a side-by-side comparison of a balcony slab drawn two ways.

Use brick red (#C95343) as the colour for heat flow arrows throughout, so the eye follows heat the same way in every panel.

Keep a safe margin of about 2% on every side. Keep Beau clear of every panel border and every important detail.

## Layout Notes

- The six panels are the whole composition. Do not add a title bar, header, footer, legend, or side panel.
- Heat arrows are always brick red, and cold surfaces are always tinted blue, so a student can follow heat across the left and right halves.
- Keep line weight, rounding, and shading (light from the upper left) consistent across the panels. The two balcony panels must use the same camera angle and the same scale.
- Keep a clean cream gutter of at least 0.8% between all panels, and a wider gutter (about 3%) down the middle between the left and right halves. Do not let arrows, shadows, or glows spill into the gutters.
- The core idea is that insulation only works if heat cannot go around it. Make the stud stripes and the unbroken slab the most striking warm elements.

## Do Not Include

- Any text, letters, numbers, labels, or captions
- A title, heading, legend, temperature scale with numbers, or banner
- Arrows with writing, signs, or readable words
- Photorealistic people or faces other than Beau
- Brand names, logos, a watermark, or a signature
