Nothing may cross a panel boundary: no shadows, glows, or stray objects in the gaps.

**Tile grid (bottom):** Nine tiles in three rows of three, each with one large, simple pictogram on a pale tint of its accent color, and nothing else. No captions. Tiles read left to right, top to bottom.

Keep a safe margin of about 2% on every side. The ring's arc proportions must be visibly unequal, with the indoor arc dominating, because that is the point of the picture.

## Layout Notes

- The ten panels are the whole composition. Do not add a title bar, header, footer, legend, or side panel.
- The nine tiles must be equal in size, evenly spaced, and share one visual style: same line weight, same corner rounding, same light direction (upper left).
- Each tile's accent color appears on its border and on one prominent object, so color helps identify it, but each tile also has a unique pictogram, so meaning never depends on color alone.
- Do not let any drawing, shadow, or glow spill into the cream gaps between panels.
- The core idea is the ring above the tiles: nearly all of the day is spent inside, so the nine tiles describe the habitat people actually live in.

## Do Not Include

- Any text, letters, numbers, labels, or captions
- A title or heading
- Hour numerals on the clock, signs with words, or arrows with text
- Photorealistic people or faces other than Beau
- Brand names or logos
- A watermark or signature
