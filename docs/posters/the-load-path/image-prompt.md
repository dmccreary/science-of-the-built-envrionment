## Layout Notes

- Markers are staggered on purpose: six sit high (about 9% to 38% from the top) and six sit low (49% to 86% from the top), so the top and bottom label strips each get half. Leave clear space at those heights.
- Keep each marker at least 5% away from its neighbors in x or y.
- Orange is reserved for the load path and used for nothing else. Red slashes and brick-red borders mark the three failures. Chestnut and tan are wood, gray is concrete, and tan-brown is soil.
- Keep line weight, rounding, and shading (light from the upper left) consistent across the house and the thumbnails.
- The core idea is that every load needs a continuous route to the ground. Make the unbroken orange path in the house and the broken orange path in each thumbnail the most striking contrast in the picture.

## Do Not Include

- Any text, letters, numbers, labels, or captions
- A title, heading, or footer
- The letter X, arrows with writing, signs, or readable words
- A comparison strip of building types, a numbered step list, or a legend with words
- Photorealistic people or faces other than Beau
- Brand names, logos, a watermark, or a signature
