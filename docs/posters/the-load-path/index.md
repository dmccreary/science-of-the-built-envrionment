## Why This Matters

Structural engineers teach that every load must have a continuous path to the ground, and that each link must be strong enough, stiff enough, and connected to the next. The poster follows one orange route from the roof to the soil, then shows three ways the route breaks: a missing uplift tie, a removed bearing wall, and a footing on soft ground.

This poster is a composite of standard structural concepts, not a single published diagram. It is simplified: real houses carry loads through many parallel paths, and gravity, wind, and earthquake loads each need their own. Use it to learn the habit of tracing a load, and rely on the adopted code and a licensed engineer for any real design or remodel.
