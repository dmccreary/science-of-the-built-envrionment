## Why This Matters

The National Institute of Building Sciences publishes the Whole Building Design Guide (WBDG), which describes whole building design as two components, an integrated design approach and an integrated team process, working toward a high-performance building. WBDG names eight design objectives: accessible, aesthetics, cost-effective, functional/operational, historic preservation, productive, secure/safe, and sustainable. Each can be critical on a given project, and WBDG's point is that they must be understood together and held in balance.

This poster is an original illustration of that idea, not a copy of the WBDG diagram. The segment order is alphabetical and carries no priority, and the six team roles are this book's examples. Definitions are paraphrased from WBDG.
