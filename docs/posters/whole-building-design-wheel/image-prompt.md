## Layout Notes

- The eight wedges are equal in size, spacing, and visual weight. Do not enlarge or emphasize any one.
- Markers are staggered on purpose: six sit high (about 20% to 45% from the top) and six sit low (about 58% to 94%). Keep the artwork clear and uncluttered at those heights so each dot lands on a distinct feature.
- Light comes from the upper left, line weight is consistent across the wedges, and wedge corners are rounded the same amount.
- Color identifies each objective, but every wedge also carries a distinct pictogram, so meaning never depends on color alone.
- The core idea is balance: a high-performance building at the hub, wrapped by how the team works, wrapped by eight equal objectives.

## Do Not Include

- Any text, letters, numbers, labels, or captions
- A title, heading, or legend
- Spokes with written tension labels, arrows with text, or signs with readable words
- Photorealistic people or faces other than Beau
- Brand names, logos, a watermark, or a signature
