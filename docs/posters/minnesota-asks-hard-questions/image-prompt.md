## Critical Rule

**This image must contain absolutely no text of any kind.** No title, no headings, no labels, no letters, no numerals, no captions, no question marks, no exclamation marks, no arrows with writing, no signs with readable writing, no logos, no watermark. The map shows no place names, county names, or zone numbers, the thermometer and gauges show only unlabeled tick marks, and the code book shows only blank covers and blank lines. Zone numbers, temperatures, and degree-day totals appear ONLY in the interactive data file. All labeling is added later by an interactive HTML overlay. If any lettering or numbers appear anywhere in the picture, regenerate.

## Image Specifications

- **Format:** PNG
- **Dimensions:** 1536 × 1024 px, landscape, 3:2 ratio
- **Background:** Warm cream (#FBF6EC) with a very faint blueprint grid
- **Audience:** College undergraduates in construction, architecture, and building-systems programs; adult but friendly
- **Style:** Clean modern flat-vector technical illustration with soft shading and a consistent line weight, saturated but professional colors, in the same family as a flat cartoon mascot. Technically accurate building components. No photorealism, no 3D render look.

## What to Draw

One poster with **two columns**. The narrow **left column** (about 2% to 31% across) holds two stacked panels: a map of Minnesota above and a climate-data panel below. The wide **right column** (about 31.4% to 98% across) holds **one house shown in cross-section, cut into five separate panels** like an exploded section: a roof and attic band at the top, a wall band under it, and below the ground line a row of three foundation panels.

Each panel has a rounded rectangle shape, a thin border in its accent color, and a pale tinted background. Panels are separated by clear cream gaps of about 0.8% so that every region is distinct. Nothing may cross a panel boundary, and drawings, shadows, and glows must stay inside their panel. The ground line runs straight across the top of the three foundation panels so they still read as parts of one house. Keep a safe margin of about 2% on every side.

Each of the five house panels carries a small **hard-question picture** in its top-left corner: a circle about 4% of the image width across, filled in the panel's accent color, containing a simple pictogram of the problem. The pictograms are described below. They contain no characters at all.

## Layout Notes

- The poster has two ideas side by side: the climate on the left, and the house that answers it on the right. Match every hard-question pictogram to its panel's accent color.
- Keep the five house panels visually continuous: same siding, same roof shingles, same line weight, and light from the upper left.
- Each house panel should have a clear, simple center of interest so a hover target reads cleanly: eave and attic, wall layers, footing and frost line, drain and steps, and basement dehumidifier.
- Between panels leave a clear cream gap. Do not let drawings, shadows, or glows spill into the gaps.
- Do not draw any number on the thermometer, dial, chart, or map. The poster teaches where to look numbers up, not what they are.

## Do Not Include

- Any text, letters, numbers, labels, or captions
- A title, heading, or legend
- Arrows with writing or signs with readable words
- Photorealistic people or faces other than Beau
- Brand names, logos, a watermark, or a signature
