## Why This Matters

In 2014 Terrapin Bright Green published *14 Patterns of Biophilic Design*, which sorts the ways people connect with nature in buildings into three groups: Nature in the Space, Natural Analogues, and Nature of the Space. A tenth-anniversary edition adds a fifteenth pattern, Awe. The poster shows nine of the fourteen original patterns: Visual Connection with Nature, Dynamic and Diffuse Light, Thermal and Airflow Variability, Presence of Water, Material Connection with Nature, Biomorphic Forms and Patterns, Prospect, Refuge, and Mystery. Five others, including Complexity and Order, are not drawn.

The patterns are a design framework, not a code requirement, and the strength of the evidence behind each one varies. The lighting strip is this book's own link to Chapters 16 and 19: daylight pays off only when the controls respond.
