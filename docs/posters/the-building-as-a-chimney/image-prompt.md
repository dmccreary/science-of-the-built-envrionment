## Layout Notes

- Keep the winter building the dominant element. The summer building and the two insets should be clearly secondary but equally clean.
- Color coding: red means warm air moving up and out, blue means cold air moving in, green means safe or controlled, gray means smoke, charcoal means the neutral pressure plane, violet means the shafts.
- The arrows must reverse direction between the winter and summer buildings, and that reversal is the second key idea. The first is the chimney itself.
- Keep line weight, corner rounding, and light direction (upper left) the same everywhere.
- Leave about 7% to 10% of clearance around each marker position so dots and leader lines read cleanly.
- The core idea is that in winter every tall building is a chimney, and the same physics that wastes heat can spread smoke.

## Do Not Include

- Any text, letters, numbers, plus or minus signs, or labels
- A title, heading, or legend
- Arrows with text or signs with readable words
- Photorealistic people or faces other than Beau
- Brand names, logos, a watermark, or a signature
