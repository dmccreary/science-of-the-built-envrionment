## Layout Notes

- The nine panels are the whole composition. Do not add a title bar, header, footer, legend, or side panel.
- The five top panels must be roughly the same width and height so the stages read as equals, with the ribbon passing through the cream gutters between them.
- The three bottom brackets must line up exactly under the stage columns they describe: the two chestnut brackets under the first three and the last stage, and the red bracket under the fourth. The red gap in the middle is what shows that operating energy is a different kind of carbon.
- Keep line weight, corner radius, and shading direction (light from the upper left) the same across all panels.
- The core idea is that a material's impact does not start at the jobsite or end at the dumpster, and that the loop from the end back to the beginning is what makes cradle to grave into cradle to cradle.
