The map's seven nodes are Structure, Enclosure, HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Fire Protection, and Occupants. Arrow thickness suggests how strongly two systems interact, and the dashed lines from Occupants show that behavior touches every system. The thickness is a teaching sketch, not a measurement. The poster's closing idea: no system is an island.

## Why This Matters

This poster is a teaching synthesis, not a published diagram. It draws on the "house as a system" framing used by the U.S. Department of Energy's Building America program, in which the envelope, the mechanical systems, and the occupants together determine performance. It also draws on research into holistic synergy analysis of building subsystems by Lumpkin, Horton, and Sinfield (2020).

Every relationship shown is a standard building-science link covered in Chapters 14, 15, 16, and 19. The ripple example shows how to follow one change through the systems, including the push-back from added cost, weight, and unchanged ventilation load. Real buildings have more links than seven nodes can show.
